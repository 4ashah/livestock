Getting Started### 1.1 System Requirements| Component | Minimum | Recommended ||---|---|---|| Browser | Chrome 120+, Edge 120+, Firefox 120+, Safari 17+ | Latest stable Chrome || Screen Resolution | 1280×720 (tablet) | 1920×1080 (desktop) || Connection | 1 Mbps broadband | 10 Mbps for large PDF/image uploads || JavaScript | Enabled (required) | Enabled || Cookies | Required for authentication session | |The application is responsive and works on tablets and mobile phones; best experience on desktop/laptop for large data entry grids (invoice lines).### 1.2 Logging In1. Navigate to the URL provided by your administrator (e.g. `https://livestock.yourcompany.com`).2. You will be redirected to the **Sign In** page automatically if not authenticated.3. Enter your **Email** or **Username** and **Password**.4. Click **Remember me** to stay signed in for 30 days on this device (only on trusted private devices; do not check on shared/public workstations).5. Click **Sign In**.**First login with a temporary password?** After submitting, the system will prompt you to choose a new permanent password (minimum 10 characters, must include uppercase letter, lowercase letter, digit, and non-alphanumeric symbol).**TOTP Two-Factor Authentication (if enabled):** If your account or role (especially SystemAdministrator) requires 2FA, after a successful password challenge enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, etc.) into the TOTP Code box.**Locked out?** Five consecutive failed login attempts lock the account for 5 minutes. Wait or contact your CompanyAdministrator to unlock early.### 1.3 Password Reset (Forgot Password)1. On the sign-in page click **Forgot your password?**2. Enter the email address associated with your account.3. Click **Send reset link**.4. Within a few minutes you will receive an email from the system with the subject "Reset your password". Click the reset link in the email.5. On the reset page enter your new password twice, then submit.6. Return to the sign-in page and log in with the new password.*If the email does not arrive:* check spam/junk folder, or ask your CompanyAdministrator to issue a new reset link.### 1.4 The Dashboard (Landing Page)After login you land on the **Dashboard**. The Dashboard contains KPI tiles (current month default, period selectable top-right):| KPI Tile | Meaning ||---|---|| **Total Sales** | Grand total of invoices with status Sent/Paid for period. || **Total Purchases** | Grand total of posted purchases for period. || **Total Expenses** | Sum of expense GrandTotal for period. || **Net Profit** | Total Sales − Total Purchases − Total Expenses (accounting-level approximation; for exact numbers run Profit & Loss Report). || **Receivables (A/R)** | Total BalanceDue across unpaid invoices. || **Payables (A/P)** | Total unpaid purchase balances. || **Livestock (Alive)** | Head count of livestock with Status = Alive (not sold/slaughtered/dead). || **Overdue Invoices** | Count of unpaid invoices whose DueDate has passed. |Below tiles = shortcut buttons (per role): New Livestock, New Invoice, Record Payment, New Expense, Reports.### 1.5 Logging OutClick your profile avatar/name in the **top-right corner** → **Sign out**. All sessions are revoked; you will need username+password again. For security, always sign out on shared workstations (the session also auto-expires after 30 minutes idle and absolutely after 12 hours).---## 2. Livestock Workflow (End-to-End)This section walks through a realistic intake → weight tracking → sale lifecycle for one head or a batch of livestock.### 3.1 Step 1: Register Livestock (Single Animal)Prerequisite: Farm must exist (if not, ask FarmManager or CompanyAdministrator to create one first).1. In sidebar click **🐄 Livestock** → list page loads.2. Top-right click **+ New Livestock**.3. Complete the form:| Field | Required | Explanation ||---|---|---|| Farm | ✔️ Pick from dropdown | Animals are per-farm || Tag ID | ✔️ Unique per company | Ear tag or visual identifier (up to 64 chars) || Type | ✔️ Ah/Su/Sa/Ad/Sd | Ah=Purchased castrated ram, Su=Uncastrated ram, Sa=Purchased ewe, Ad=Bred castrated ram, Sd=Bred ewe (ovine/sheep only) || Date of Birth | Optional | Use date picker || Gender | ✔️ M / F / Unknown | || Breed | Optional | Free-text, e.g. "Holstein", "Large White" || Initial Weight | ✔️ Number | Numeric value || Weight Unit | ✔️ kg or lb | Stored internally as kg regardless || Sire Tag ID | Optional | Father animal (lookup) || Dam Tag ID | Optional | Mother animal (lookup) || Photo | Optional | Allow: jpg/jpeg/png only, max 10 MB || Notes | Optional | Any relevant info |4. Click **Save**.*Result:* A green toast appears "Livestock {TagId} created". The list now contains the new row; Dashboard "Livestock (Alive)" counter increments by 1.### 3.2 Step 2: Register Livestock (Batch CSV Import) — For Large Herds1. Livestock list page → **⚙ Bulk** dropdown → **Import CSV**.2. Download the CSV template (button provided on the dialog).3. Fill the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets with columns: `TagId, Type (Ah/Su/Sa/Ad/Sd), DOB (yyyy-mm-dd), Gender (M/F/U), Breed, Weight, WeightUnit (kg/lb), FarmCode`.4. Save as CSV (UTF-8 with BOM recommended).5. Upload the file.6. The system validates all rows. If ANY row is invalid, **the entire batch is rejected** with per-row error messages shown (row number, field, problem). Fix errors and re-upload.7. If all valid → confirm the dialog; all animals are registered atomically.### 3.3 Step 3: Add Weight Entry (Regular Weigh-Ins)1. Open Livestock detail → click animal TagId row link in list.2. Tab **Weight History** → click **+ Record Weight**.3. Enter:   - **Date & Time** of weighing (defaults to now).   - **Weight** + **Unit** (kg/lb).   - **Weighed By** defaults to you.   - **Notes** optional.4. **Save**. Weight entries are immutable — if you made a mistake, record a CORRECTION entry with a note explaining the reversal instead of editing. The Current Weight displayed at the top of the detail page always reflects the most recent entry.### 3.4 Step 4: Mark Lifecycle Event — Death / Slaughter (Not Sold)Only FarmManager+ may do this (or CompanyAdministrator):1. Livestock detail → top-right **Status** dropdown → change **Alive** → **Dead** or **Slaughtered**.2. If **Slaughtered**: fill optional Slaughter Batch Reference.3. If **Dead**: fill Cause of Death.4. Confirm. Status change is IRREVERSIBLE. Dashboard Alive counter decreases by 1.*To sell an animal for money, do NOT use these statuses — follow §5 Sales Invoicing instead, which auto-marks Sold on payment.*---## 4. Purchases (Buying Animals from Suppliers)### 4.1 Create a Purchase1. Sidebar → **📥 Purchases** → **+ New Purchase**.2. Header:| Field | Default | Notes ||---|---|---|| Supplier | — | Create from 🧑 Suppliers if missing || Purchase Date | Today | || Destination Farm | — | Animals will be registered to this farm || Supplier Invoice No | — | Optional, supplier's invoice number || Header Discount % | 0.00 | Applied to all lines (editable per line) || Tax Rate % | Company default (20%) | Editable || Prices include tax? | Off | On → prices are gross; net calculated by system |3. Add **Line Items** — click **+ Add Line**:   - LivestockType (Ah/Su/Sa/Ad/Sd)   - Quantity (head count)   - Avg Unit Weight + Unit   - Unit Price (per head)   - Line Discount % and Tax Rate override if needed4. Totals block updates live.5. Click **Save as Draft** to come back, or **Post** to finalize.**Posting is irreversible.** When you click **Post**, the system automatically creates individual livestock records for you (one per head, with sequential tag IDs like `{PurchaseNumber}-001-001`, `{PurchaseNumber}-001-002`, ...) and one weight entry each matching the line's average weight. Stock-on-hand on the farm is incremented. If you provided a Tag ID list instead, those are used.### 4.2 Attach a Supplier Invoice DocumentOn Purchase detail → **Attachments** tab → drag-drop or click to upload supplier's PDF/scanned invoice. Allow-list: `.pdf, .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .csv` (max 10 MB each). Scanned documents are always retrievable later for audits.---## 5. Sales & Invoicing Workflow### 5.1 Create a Sales Order (Optional Step — Quotes/Confirmations)You can skip sales orders and create invoices directly if you don't need a confirmation step.1. **📤 Sales Orders** → **+ New Sales Order**.2. Pick Customer (create from 🧑 Customers tab if needed; ensure Payment Terms on customer are correct first).3. Pick Farm (where the animals are currently penned; customer PO number optional).4. Add lines using **+ Specific Head** (pick specific livestock by TagId one by one) OR **+ Batch Line** (enter LivestockType + Quantity without assigning individual heads yet — useful for small batches where per-head tracking is not needed).5. Enter Unit Price, line discounts. Totals calculate.6. Save as Draft → then **Confirm** (only Accounts/CompanyAdministrator role). Once Confirmed it becomes eligible for invoicing.### 5.2 Generate an Invoice from a Sales Order1. Open Confirmed Sales Order → top-right action **⚡ Generate Invoice**.2. A Draft Invoice pre-fills from the Sales Order. Verify, change DueDate if needed (default 30 days from InvoiceDate per company default), check billing address snapshot (editable, frozen after Send).3. Click **Preview PDF** to verify the invoice document visually before sending.### 5.3 Create an Invoice Directly (No Sales Order)Sidebar **🧾 Invoices** → **+ New Invoice** → fill Customer, Due Date, add lines directly. Same preview step.### 5.4 Send Invoice to CustomerFrom Draft Invoice detail: **📧 Send Invoice**.What happens behind the scenes:1. Status changes from **Draft** → **Sent**.2. Final PDF is generated and archived (tamper-evident hash stored).3. An email is queued in the background (Hangfire). Subject: `Invoice {InvoiceNumber} from {YourCompanyName}`. Recipient = Customer.Email. The PDF is attached.4. You will see a toast: "Invoice email queued for sending". You can see the exact send status on the invoice detail page: Last sent, attempts, any delivery errors. If sending fails temporarily (e.g. email provider down), it retries automatically 3 times with exponential backoff.To send many invoices at once: list page → multi-select checkboxes → **⚙ Bulk** → **Send via Email**.### 5.5 Download a PDFFrom invoice detail: **⬇ Download PDF**. Always reflects the snapshot; customer editing/changes will not change this PDF.### 5.6 Void an Invoice (Wrong Bill, Returns)If you sent an invoice with errors:1. Invoice detail → top-right **Void**.2. Enter **Void Reason** (required for audit, 10 char minimum).3. Confirm Void.Status becomes **Voided**. Numbers are NOT reused (tax authority requirement — gap-less numbering with voids retained in the ledger for auditors). By default any livestock already marked Sold due to this invoice are REVERTED to Alive on void (configurable by company policy).*If customer needs a reduction, do NOT void. Instead issue a Credit Note (stub feature) for the correction amount.*### 5.7 Overdue InvoicesOn invoice list, the **Overdue** badge is shown automatically when today's date is past the Due Date and the balance is still owed. From invoice detail you can **📧 Send Reminder** (Accounts role or higher).---## 6. Payments & Receipts### 6.1 Record a Customer Payment (Incoming Money)1. Sidebar → **💳 Payments** → **+ New Payment**.2. Fill:| Field | Notes ||---|---|| Customer | Who paid you || Payment Date | When received || Payment Method | Cash / Bank Transfer / Check / Card / Online || Reference No | Check number, transaction ID || Amount | Amount received || Deposited into account | Optional free-text bank account reference || Notes | |3. Choose allocation strategy:   - **AUTO (Oldest Due First)** button → system fills allocations automatically, starting with the most overdue invoices, then next, until payment amount is fully allocated.   - **Manual**: click on the invoice rows in the allocations grid; type allocation amounts per row. Total must equal Payment Amount.   - **Overpayment**: if customer paid more than owed (common with rounding), remainder creates a Credit Note which will auto-apply to the next invoice(s).4. **Save**.*Result:* As each invoice's balance reaches zero (within 0.01 tolerance), its status flips from **Sent** → **Paid**, and any livestock linked to that invoice get Status updated to **Sold** automatically.### 6.2 Record a Supplier Receipt (Money You Paid Out)Sidebar **🧾 Receipts** → **+ New Receipt**. Flow is analogous to payments, but allocates against Posted Purchases, reducing Accounts Payable (A/P).---## 7. ReportsAll reports are accessed via the sidebar **📈 Reports** menu. Troubleshooting & Support### Frequently Asked Questions**Q: I can't see a menu item?** → It's likely you don't have the required role. Contact your CompanyAdministrator to check your role assignments.**Q: I click Save but get red validation errors?** → Scroll up; the failing fields are highlighted in red with error messages below. Fix and re-submit.**Q: Invoice PDF shows wrong company logo?** → Only CompanyAdministrator can change the logo (Company Settings → Logo upload).**Q: The invoice number sequence jumped (gap)?** → This happens when someone saves an invoice as Draft and then Voids it (the sequence number is consumed at Draft creation time, not at send time, to support auditors). This is normal and correct per tax rules. Voided invoices remain searchable with their Void status and reason in the system forever.**Q: File upload fails "invalid file type"?** → Make sure the extension is in the allow-list (pdf/jpg/jpeg/png/doc/docx/xls/xlsx/csv) AND the actual file content matches. If you renamed a `.exe` to `.pdf`, the system detects the mismatch by checking file magic bytes.**Q: Dashboard numbers don't match the report?** → Dashboard uses MTD default; the report uses whatever date range you last selected. Align the periods and re-compare. Small differences ≤ 0.02 can happen due to rounding — re-run Profit & Loss report with exact same date range for authoritative numbers.### Escalation Path for Problems1. First, check this guide for FAQs.2. Report internally to your CompanyAdministrator. Provide:   - Exact URL and steps to reproduce (ideally a short screen recording or screenshot).   - Date/time the error occurred (approx is fine — logs are timestamped in UTC).   - Error message text, if any.   - Your username / role.3. CompanyAdministrator escalates to SystemAdministrator or hosting/vendor support with additional information from logs (Audit Logs viewable to SystemAdministrator).4. Production outage? SystemAdministrator can immediately roll back to the prior deployed version using the Backup & Rollback Procedure (see ADMIN_GUIDE.md).
