## Company Administrator & System Administrator Playbook---## 1. Administrator Roles OverviewThis guide covers two elevated roles.### 1.1 CompanyAdministrator (Per-Company)- Scope: **One single company only**.- What they manage within their company:  - Farms (create/edit/delete, assign managers)  - Users (invite, assign roles up to Accounts/FarmManager, disable, force password reset)  - Company profile (name, legal address, logo, tax rates, invoice defaults, payment terms, due days, currencies—after creation Currency becomes immutable)  - Custom expense categories, document templates (invoice email subject/body templates), bank info display on invoices  - Quotas & storage usage viewer  - Document voiding of any kind  - Audit logs for their company (subset)- What they CANNOT do:  - Create or delete other companies  - See data from other companies  - Grant roles with higher rank than CompanyAdministrator (e.g. SystemAdministrator)  - Access the Hangfire dashboard  - Cross-company reports  - Modify document sequence numbers directly (only break-glass by SystemAdministrator)### 1.2 SystemAdministrator (Global)- Scope: **All companies**, plus infrastructure-level configuration.- Added capabilities:  - Create/edit/delete (soft-delete) companies, assign CompanyAdministrators, set quotas  - Impersonate any company context (company switcher dropdown)  - Manage any user in any company (unlock, MFA reset, force-disable, password reset, cross-company role changes)  - View global audit logs across all companies with full filters  - Access Hangfire dashboard (`/hangfire`) to monitor email queues, PDF jobs, retries, dead-letters; manually retry jobs  - System-level settings: global file storage quotas, allow-list extensions, rate limit thresholds (stored in `AppSettings`)  - System health dashboard, `/health` output, storage usage reports per company  - Trigger DB migrations (or view status) during deployments  - Break-glass actions: reset document counters, manually set custom invoice numbers (logged heavily with reason), enable/disable maintenance mode---## 2. Company Setup (First-Time Onboarding)### 2.1 Step 1: Create the Company (SystemAdministrator)1. Log in as **SystemAdministrator**.2. Sidebar → **🏢 Companies** → **+ New Company**.3. Fill the company registration form:| Field | Required | Guidance ||---|---|---|| Name | ✔ | Legal name; displayed on invoices || Registration Number / Tax ID | Optional | VAT/CIF/FEIN; printed on invoices || Legal Address | ✔ | All 6 fields || Contact Email / Phone | Optional | || Currency | ✔ | USD or EUR — IMMUTABLE after first posted document || Default Tax Rate % | ✔ | Default 20.00; 0.00–35.00 || Default Weight Unit | ✔ | kg or lb || Default Invoice Due Days | ✔ | Default 30 || Time Zone | ✔ | IANA ID (e.g. America/New_York, Europe/London) — timestamps display to users in this zone || Logo | Optional | PNG/JPG, square or 3:1, will be scaled on letterhead; max 5 MB || Sender Email (From-address) | Optional | `invoices@yourcompany.com`; must have SPF/DKIM set with your email provider || Bank info | Optional | Printed on invoice footer for wire payments |4. **Save** → green toast "Company created".### 2.2 Step 2: Create a CompanyAdministrator User for the Tenant1. Open company details → **Users** tab → **+ Invite User**.2. Enter **Email** + **Full Name**.3. Role dropdown: select **CompanyAdministrator** (highest per-company role).4. Optional: message field in the invite email body.5. Click **Send Invitation**.The new admin receives an email with a one-time link to set their password (link valid 24 hours). Once they set their password, they can log in and complete remaining company configuration themselves.### 2.3 Step 3: CompanyAdministrator Completes Company ProfileCompany admin logs in. Sidebar → **⚙ Company Settings**:1. **Profile** tab: review legal info, bank info, logo.2. **Tax & Billing** tab: confirm Default Tax Rate, Default Invoice Due Days, Currency (locked), Prices-Include-Tax toggle.3. **Invoice Appearance** tab: customize invoice PDF footer notes, default subject line and email body template (Razor-style variables `{{InvoiceNumber}}`, `{{DueDate}}`, `{{Balance}}`), terms and conditions on PDF.4. **Expense Categories** tab: default categories (Feed, Vet, Transport, Utilities, Labor, Other) are present. Add custom categories specific to your business (e.g. "Insurance", "Fuel", "Equipment Rental").5. **Numbering** tab: view current counters per document type (Purchases, Invoices, etc.) and year. Read-only for CompanyAdmin; SystemAdministrator can do a break-glass "Set counter" if migrating from legacy.6. **Storage Usage** tab: shows current GB used vs quota (default 50 GB); request increase from SystemAdministrator once hitting 80%.### 2.4 Step 4: Add FarmsCompanyAdmin or later delegated FarmManager → **🚜 Farms**:For each physical farm site:- **Name** (e.g. "Willow Creek North Pasture")- **Code** — short unique code per company (e.g. "WCN") used on reports.- Full address + GPS latitude/longitude optional.- Assign a **Manager User** (FarmManager role user you will invite in next step).- Area in hectares optional, notes.### 2.5 Step 5: Invite Users and Assign RolesCompany **⚙ Users & Permissions** → **+ Invite User**. Fill email, full name, pick a role from:| Role | When to Assign ||---|---|| **Viewer** | Auditor, non-editing owner, board member read-only — NO export, NO edits. || **DataEntry** | Seasonal worker entering weights/purchases/expenses, can draft invoices. Cannot void, cannot delete. || **FarmManager** | Senior farm staff. Livestock lifecycle, batch imports, weight entries, exports. Cannot touch Accounts Payable/Receivable (no voids). || **Accounts** | Bookkeeper/accountant. Sales orders, invoices, payments/receipts, financial reports (P&L, A/R, A/P). Can void invoices. Cannot manage users. || **CompanyAdministrator** | Owner/GM. Full control within one company. Invite users, assign other roles, everything except cross-company. |*Important:* always apply **principle of least privilege** — start Viewer, escalate as needed.---## 3. User & Role Management (Day-to-Day)### 3.1 Unlock a Locked-Out UserUser gets "This account has been locked out" after 5 failed passwords.1. **⚙ Users & Permissions** → find user row.2. Status column shows 🚫 Locked.3. Click row actions → **Unlock** (or open user → Security tab → Unlock button).4. Optional: click **Send password reset** if they forgot their password.### 3.2 Force Password Change on Next LoginUser → detail → **Security** → check **Force password change on next login** → Save. Next login forces reset.### 3.3 Reset TOTP 2FA for a User Who Lost Their PhoneUser → detail → **Security** → **Reset 2FA (TOTP)**. Confirm with reason. Next time user logs in they are required to re-enroll TOTP from scratch.### 3.4 Disable a User (Offboarding)User → detail → check **Account Enabled** toggle **Off** → Save. User cannot log in; audit records preserved (no hard-delete). You may also set End Date for records.### 3.5 Change User RoleUser → **Roles** tab. Uncheck old role, check new role → Save. Role change is effective on next login (cookie claims re-issued). Audit log entry created with reason required.### 3.6 Audit Role Assignments**⚙ Users & Permissions** → top-right **Export Role Assignments CSV**. Gives you User, Email, Roles, Enabled, LastLogin. Review quarterly and revoke stale accounts.---## 4. Farm Management### 4.1 Merge Farms (Restructuring)If you consolidate two farms:1. In source farm → **Livestock** → Bulk select all alive livestock → bulk action **Change Farm → target farm**.2. Verify all livestock moved (Dashboard counts adjust).3. Ensure no active livestock remain.4. Edit Farm → toggle **IsActive = false** (soft-disable; keeps history) or soft-delete with reason.### 4.2 Assign New Farm ManagerFarms → edit → change Manager User dropdown to another user who has FarmManager (or higher) role assigned. Save. The new manager now sees that farm in their default filters.---## 5. System Settings (SystemAdministrator Only)### 5.1 Adjust Storage QuotaCompany detail → Quota tab. Edit **Storage Quota GB** field, save. If a company exceeds quota, uploads begin failing with 413 "quota exceeded" HTTP error. Best practice: warn at 80% (the dashboard shows admins a warning banner at 80% usage).### 5.2 Break-Glass: Reset Document CounterUsed for migration from legacy or to correct a mis-seeded counter. **WARNING:** changing counters can break tax audit requirements — use ONLY during initial data migration, never for active companies after go-live.1. Companies → Numbering tab (SystemAdministrator sees Edit pencil next to each counter).2. Click pencil → type new NEXT value (e.g. 42 for a YY-00042 number on next document).3. REQUIRED field: **Reason for change** (20+ characters saved to AuditLogs with your user id).4. Save → counter reset validated (cannot set to lower than last actually issued number — that would create duplicates which are blocked by unique key).### 5.3 Break-Glass: Manually Set Invoice NumberUsed for rare corrections when migrating historical invoices. Action: Invoice detail → SystemAdministrator sees **Set Custom Number** button. Provide new custom Invoice number (must match YY-NNNNN format or allowed pattern + be unique per company). Reason required. Logged heavily.### 5.4 Hangfire DashboardURL `/hangfire` (auto-hidden route, SystemAdministrator role only).Sections:- **Jobs / Queued**: pending email sends, PDF generations.- **Processing**: in-flight.- **Succeeded**: recent successes (30-day retention).- **Failed**: red jobs → retry manually or delete after root cause.- **Recurring Jobs**: monthly recurring invoices, file deletion (cleanup old soft-deleted StoredFiles), audit log archive, Hangfire dashboard cleanup.If emails stop sending:1. Check `/hangfire → Failed` tab. Red entries show the exception (e.g. SendGrid 401 Bad API Key).2. Fix config — for deployments: change env var `Email__SendGridApiKey` and recycle App Pool.3. Requeue failed jobs with **Retry** button.### 5.5 Audit Log ReviewTop navbar SystemAdministrator → **Audit Logs** page. Filter:- Date range- Company (dropdown)- Entity Type (Invoice, Livestock, User, …)- Entity ID- Operation (Create / Update / Delete / Void)- User- Changed columns searchOpen row for JSON diff viewer Old Values ↔ New Values. Export to CSV for compliance auditors.### 5.6 Maintenance ModeTop bar **System** → **Enable Maintenance Mode**. Puts site in read-only for everyone except SystemAdministrator: all POST endpoints return 503 with maintenance page. Use during version upgrades or large migrations. Deactivate when done.---## 6. Backup & Restore### 6.1 Backup TypesYour backup strategy (DEPLOYMENT §10) should cover:| Data | Frequency | Retention | How Backed Up ||---|---|---|---|| SQL Server Database (full) | Nightly 02:00 | 7 daily + 4 weekly + 12 monthly | SQL Server Agent job / Azure SQL automated backups || Transaction log backup | Every 15 min | Same as full | TLOG backups for point-in-time || File Attachments (Local disk) | Nightly 03:00 | 7+4+12 months | Robocopy D:\files\… → NAS/secondary storage || Hangfire SQL Server schema | Covered by full DB backup | | Same as DB || Data Protection Keys | Hourly snapshot | 12 months | Copy `AppData\keys` → backup || Serilog logs | Archived after 30 days | 12 months online + 7 years cold blob | Serilog rolling file sink → archive storage |### 6.2 Point-in-Time Database Restore (Step-by-Step)Scenario: a user accidentally voided 30 invoices at 10:12 AM; we need to restore the state at 10:11 AM (the minute before the incident).1. **Take tail-log backup** of current production (so we can replay forward if needed):   ```sql   BACKUP LOG LivestockManagement TO DISK = N'D:\Backups\Livestock_tail_t1200.trn' WITH NORECOVERY, NO_TRUNCATE;   ```2. **Restore full backup** (last night's 02:00 full) WITH NORECOVERY into a *staging* database (not on top of prod yet — keep prod live):   ```sql   RESTORE DATABASE LivestockManagement_Staging   FROM DISK = N'D:\Backups\Livestock_FULL_20260806_0200.bak'   WITH NORECOVERY, REPLACE,   MOVE 'LivestockManagement' TO 'D:\SQLData\LivestockManagement_Staging.mdf',   MOVE 'LivestockManagement_log' TO 'D:\SQLLogs\LivestockManagement_Staging_log.ldf';   ```3. **Apply differential** (if any) + every T-log backup between full and 10:11 AM with `NORECOVERY`:   ```sql   RESTORE LOG LivestockManagement_Staging FROM DISK = N'D:\Backups\Livestock_LOG_0945.trn' WITH NORECOVERY;   RESTORE LOG LivestockManagement_Staging FROM DISK = N'D:\Backups\Livestock_LOG_1000.trn' WITH NORECOVERY;   RESTORE LOG LivestockManagement_Staging FROM DISK = N'D:\Backups\Livestock_LOG_1015.trn'     WITH STOPAT = N'2026-08-06T10:11:00', NORECOVERY;   ```4. Finalize staging DB:   ```sql   RESTORE DATABASE LivestockManagement_Staging WITH RECOVERY;   ```5. **Compare prod vs staging** the 30 voided invoices using a cross-db SQL script — pull void-revert script, then run an UPDATE on prod to un-void them (manual surgical fix preferred over full prod restore if incident is isolated).6. If full restore is truly required (e.g. ransomware, widespread data corruption), coordinate with downtime window: enable Maintenance Mode → restore to prod from staging via backup/restore cycle → disable Maintenance Mode → smoke test → communicate to tenants.### 6.3 File Attachments Restore- Local storage: restore folder tree from backup copy at `D:\inetpub\wwwroot\livestock\files\…` preserving GUID filenames.- If the StoredFiles table records were also deleted, restore DB first to a staging database, sync table rows to prod, then restore files.### 6.4 Quarterly Restore DrillThe **SystemAdministrator** must perform a restore drill at least once per quarter (or per internal compliance requirement):1. Pick random day T-30.2. Restore database + files to a test environment.3. End-to-end smoke: login as a test user, view a report, download an old invoice, pull up one of the last 20 livestock added before T-30.4. Sign-off: log `[restore-drill YYYY-MM-DD] PASS/FAIL + notes` to a runbook. Retain signatures for 7 years auditable.---## 7. Version Upgrade Procedure### 7.1 Pre-Release Checklist (Before deploy)1. All tickets in release → passed QA, unit + integration tests green on CI.2. Security scan 0 Critical/High.3. Migration script reviewed by DBA for destructive changes (DROPs must be blocked in v1 unless explicitly signed off).4. **Backups taken fresh** (manual on-demand full backup right before upgrade window, separate from nightly).5. Maintenance Mode ready to enable.6. Communicated downtime window to users (email blast + banner).### 7.2 Deployment Steps (Production IIS)1. **Download deployment artifacts** from CI: `Livestock.Web.<BuildId>.zip` + `migrations.sql`.2. **Maintenance Mode ON** (SystemAdministrator UI).3. **AppPool Stop** briefly → or rely on App_Offline.htm trick.4. **Run migrations** against production DB using `sqlcmd` or SSMS:   ```powershell   sqlcmd -S DB-SERVER-01 -d LivestockManagement -U deploy_admin -P "…" -i .\migrations.sql -b   ```   `-b` = exit on first error so the deploy stops instead of half-migrated.5. **Backup prior web files**: rename `www` folder → `www-prev-YYYYMMDD-HHMM`; unzip new build into `www`.6. **Verify env vars**: confirm `ASPNETCORE_ENVIRONMENT=Production` + `ENV_ENABLE_DEV_SEED=false` are STILL SET (some deploy tools overwrite these).7. **Start or Warm Up AppPool**:   ```powershell   Start-WebAppPool LivestockAppPool   curl -k https://localhost/health  → Healthy   ```8. Smoke test by SystemAdministrator:   - Login works.   - Dashboard loads (KPIs populated).   - Open any existing invoice, download PDF (works).   - Create a test Purchase Draft and save it → delete it.   - /hangfire shows no failed jobs startup.9. Maintenance Mode OFF.10. Notify users release complete.### 7.3 Rollback on Failure Detection (within 15 minutes window)1. Re-enable Maintenance Mode.2. **Restore code** folder: delete `www` → rename `www-prev…` → `www`.3. **Roll back migrations?** — if migrations were forward-compatible (only adds, no drops), the prior code continues to work and no DB rollback needed. If migration was destructive, restore point-in-time per §6.2.4. Warm up site → smoke test → Maintenance Mode OFF → rollback communicated as incident.5. Post-mortem mandatory; write up learnings, improve tests. Root cause fixed before next attempt.---## 8. Troubleshooting Production Issues### Symptom → Cause → Resolution Quick Reference| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix ||---|---|---|| **All users HTTP 500.30 Start-up failure after deploy** | Missing env var `ConnectionStrings__LivestockDb` | AppCmd set env var; recycle app pool; view `logs/stdout*` || **Login page works but after login redirects back to login in loop** | Antiforgery cookie mismatch; DataProtection keys rotated/lost | Check cookies SameSite/Secure; ensure DP keys persist to stable path not temp dir || **Emails queued but never sent; Hangfire shows SendGrid 401** | Invalid or rotated SendGrid API key | Update env var + recycle; /hangfire retry failed || **Invoice PDF download blank; 10% of PDFs have missing glyphs for accented chars** | Font not installed on server (DejaVu Sans missing) | Deploy font files as embedded resources OR install font family on Windows Server || **Uploads 403 forbidden suddenly** | Company hit storage quota (check Company Settings → Storage) OR file extension not in allow-list | Either bump quota OR tell user to convert file type (e.g. BMP → PNG) || **SystemAdministrator Hangfire dashboard 403** | Role claim not in cookie | Sign out / sign back in so cookie re-issues new SystemAdministrator claims || **Dashboard KPI "Livestock Alive" doesn't match Livestock list filter count** | Denormalized CurrentWeightKg refresh trigger disabled or broken. Check SQL Server → Livestock table → trigger `trg_Livestock_UpdateCurrentWeight`. Or re-enable. || **Overdue invoices badge count not decrementing after payments** | Cache for badge (if used) stale; nightly batch recalc; or manual refresh Dashboard → ⟳ Refresh button. || **Can't create a new purchase — "sequence counter" error** | `DocumentSequences` row for year missing for this company. SystemAdministrator Numbering tab → next value ensure >0. || **User cannot export CSV — HTTP 403** | They have Viewer role. Elevate to DataEntry+ (least privilege: grant role and check export policy). || **Concurrency conflict "Record modified by another user" toast** | Two users editing same invoice simultaneously. Have user reload page, re-apply their changes. Normal; no data lost. |### Log Locations| Log | Path | Contents ||---|---|---|| Serilog Application | `D:\inetpub\wwwroot\livestock\logs\livestock-YYYYMMDD.log` | All app events: unhandled exceptions, warnings, request summary, login failures, audit events (if also logged here). || ASP.NET Core Module (IIS) ANCM | `D:\inetpub\wwwroot\livestock\logs\ancm.log` | Startup failures, in-process hosting errors, module loading issues. || Hangfire | In `Hangfire.State` table; also `/hangfire UI` | Background jobs and failures. || Windows Event Log → Application | Event Viewer | .NET Runtime exceptions, AppPool recycles, IIS pipeline errors. || SQL Server Error Log | SSMS → Management → SQL Server Logs | Deadlocks, DB connection failures, backup success/failure. |When opening a vendor support ticket, always include:- UTC timestamp of occurrence (±10 minutes).- Username and role experiencing the issue.- URL(s) involved.- Screenshots of UI errors (including browser console F12 red errors).- A 10-minute snippet of Serilog log around the timestamp.---## 9.
